Company

Built in the UAE.
Built for the UAE.

Makeen Robotics LLC is a UAE-based company trading, implementing, and servicing robots and humanoids across the hospitality, F&B, healthcare, and retail sectors of the Gulf.

The name مَكِين — a Quranic Arabic word meaning "powerful and firmly established" — reflects our founding conviction: that robotics in the UAE is not a future trend but a present necessity, and that the companies who deploy it now will be firmly established when the market matures.

Mission
Make autonomous robotics accessible, implementable, and serviceable for every hospitality and service business in the UAE.
Vision
A UAE where robots handle the repetitive — so people can focus on the irreplaceable.
Backed by
UAE-based founders, seed investors, and strategic partners with deep roots in the Gulf's hospitality and investment landscape.
Partners
